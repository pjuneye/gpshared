Privacy policy

Effective and last updated: 7 October 2026
Developer: NewPower Studios · Package: com.newpower.apkshare.pro

This policy describes ApkShare Pro’s current release configuration and this support website. Contact us at pjuneye@gmail.com with questions or privacy requests.

App and package information

ApkShare Pro reads installed-app names, package identifiers, versions, APK members and integrity information on your device to show apps you can select, prepare installation packages, and check compatibility. It also processes installation files that you import, authorise in a library folder, or find through a scan that you start.

Scanning searches shared storage for supported installation-package files. It does not access another app’s private data or continuously monitor shared storage in the background. App inventories, search terms, file paths and package inspection results are not uploaded to developer or advertising servers.

Sharing between devices

When you select files and confirm a native device-to-device transfer, the receiving device obtains the selected packages and the app information needed to display them. Native transfers use an authenticated, encrypted connection and confirmation by the participants. Recipients control delivered copies; stopping a connection cannot recall files already received.

If you use Android’s system share sheet, the system component or app you select handles that sharing under its own privacy practices. Check the recipient and content before sharing.

The helper for installing ApkShare Pro on another device can temporarily serve Pro’s own installation package on the local network. This browser-download route uses local HTTP and is separate from the encrypted native-transfer route. Use it only on a trusted local network. Temporary services, tokens and connection credentials are cleared according to their session lifecycle. We do not claim that every available sharing route is encrypted.

Permissions

  • Installed-app visibility supports the local app picker, package preparation and user-initiated sharing.
  • Camera access scans connection QR codes. The app does not use it to save photos or record video.
  • Nearby Wi-Fi devices, and location permissions where Android requires them for Wi-Fi connection information, support temporary device connections. They are not used to determine your geographical location.
  • All-files access supports an installation-package scan that you explicitly start. File selection and authorised folders remain available if you decline it.
  • Notifications and a foreground service display and maintain active transfers that you start, with a stop control.
  • Permission to install unknown apps allows selected, verified packages to be handed to Android’s system installer. Installation still requires system and user confirmation.

You can revoke runtime permissions and special access in Android Settings. Forgetting a library-folder authorisation releases access without deleting that folder’s files.

Local storage and deletion

Received assets are stored locally in app-private storage until you delete them or uninstall the app. Local task records may include selected-item metadata, states, times, result codes and file references needed for activity and recovery. These records are not uploaded to developer servers. Fixed-category local usage counters are not sent to a remote analytics service.

You may delete eligible completed activity records or use the retention controls provided in Settings. Temporary partial files and recovery checkpoints follow their recovery and cleanup lifecycle.

Uninstalling removes app-private data under Android’s rules. Files that you export to document folders, other apps or another device must be deleted at those destinations.

Accounts and Google Play purchases

There is no developer app account or cloud sync. Version 0.1.1 and later use Google Play Billing for an optional lifetime unlock. Google handles the Play account, checkout and payment information. The app reads product information and purchase status and acknowledges completed purchases; it does not receive your card details. A local entitlement flag is stored outside Android backup. Restore purchases using the Google Play account that made the purchase. Pending purchases do not grant access.

Advertising and privacy choices

Internal-test version 0.1.2 includes Google Mobile Ads and User Messaging Platform (UMP), configured with Google demonstration ad units. Earlier version 0.1.0 included neither online billing nor advertising. Test ads still contact Google services. Google’s SDK may process IP addresses, ad interactions, diagnostics and device identifiers such as App Set ID. An IP address may indicate approximate location. The app requests non-personalised ads, removes the Android advertising-ID permission and disables the publisher first-party identifier; these measures do not eliminate all SDK data processing.

The app checks applicable privacy choices before requesting ads and provides privacy options where required by UMP. App inventories, selected-file names, paths, package contents and transfer details are not supplied to the ad SDK. Rewarded ads are optional; a reward applies to the current batch only after the SDK reports completion. Local reward records track an opaque batch identifier, count and consumption state. Google processes its services under Google’s privacy policy.

There is no developer-operated remote usage analytics or crash-reporting backend. Local counters are separate from Google’s advertising measurement. Uninstalling removes local app data under Android’s rules, but does not delete Google’s purchase or service records; manage those through Google’s account and privacy controls.

Support messages and this website

If you email support, we receive the address, message and any attachments you choose to provide. We use them to respond and investigate your request. Our email service processes messages to deliver and store them. Please avoid passwords, payment credentials, complete app inventories and connection QR codes. You can request deletion of support correspondence at the same address; information is retained only as needed to handle the request, resolve related issues or meet applicable legal requirements.

This website contains no advertising or analytics scripts and no forms for uploading files. GitHub Pages and its infrastructure providers process technical request information, such as IP address, request time and browser information, to deliver and protect the website. This website processing is separate from the app’s on-device processing. External links and your email application follow their own providers’ practices.

Children

ApkShare Pro is a general-purpose package-management utility, not a service designed specifically for children. We do not knowingly solicit children’s personal information through support. A parent or guardian can contact us to request deletion of information a child has sent us.

Changes and contact

We will update this page when data practices change and provide additional in-app information or consent where required. For a privacy question, access request or deletion request, email pjuneye@gmail.com.

隐私政策

生效及更新日期：2026 年 10 月 7 日
开发者：NewPower Studios · 包名：com.newpower.apkshare.pro

本政策说明 ApkShare Pro 当前发行配置及本支持网站的数据处理。隐私问题和请求请发送至 pjuneye@gmail.com。

应用与安装包信息

ApkShare Pro 在本机读取已安装应用的名称、包标识、版本、APK 成员和完整性信息，用于应用选择、安装包准备和兼容性检查。应用也会处理您通过系统选择器导入、在资料库目录中授权访问，或主动扫描找到的安装文件。

本机扫描查找共享存储中的支持格式安装文件，不访问其他应用私有数据，也不持续在后台监视共享存储。应用清单、搜索内容、文件路径和安装包检查结果不会上传到开发者或广告服务器。

设备间分享

您选择文件并确认原生设备间传输后，接收设备会获得所选安装包和展示所需的应用信息。原生传输采用经过认证的加密连接，并由参与双方确认。对方控制已经送达的副本；停止连接不能撤回已经接收的文件。

使用 Android 系统分享面板时，您选择的系统组件或其他应用会按照其自身规则处理分享。发送前请检查内容和接收方。

帮助另一台设备安装 ApkShare Pro 的功能可临时在本地网络提供 Pro 自身安装包下载。该浏览器下载使用本地 HTTP，与原生加密传输是不同路线；请仅在可信本地网络中使用。临时服务、令牌和连接凭据按会话生命周期清理。我们不声称全部分享路线均已加密。

权限用途

  • 应用可见性：支持本机应用选择、安装包准备及您发起的分享。
  • 相机：扫描连接二维码，不用于保存照片或录像。
  • 附近 Wi-Fi 设备，以及 Android 获取 Wi-Fi 连接信息所要求的位置权限：支持临时设备连接，不用于确定您的地理位置。
  • 所有文件访问：仅支持您主动发起的安装包扫描。拒绝后仍可使用文件选择和已授权目录。
  • 通知与前台服务：展示并维持您启动的活动传输，提供停止入口。
  • 安装未知应用：将所选且经过验证的安装包交给 Android 安装器，安装仍须由系统和您确认。

您可以在 Android 设置中撤销运行时权限及特殊访问。忘记资料库目录授权只释放访问权限，不会删除目录中的文件。

本地保存与删除

已接收资产保存在本地应用私有存储中，直到您删除或卸载应用。本地任务记录可包含所选项目元数据、状态、时间、结果码，以及恢复任务所需的文件引用，用于活动展示及恢复；这些记录不会上传到开发者服务器。固定类别的本地使用汇总不会发送到远程分析服务。

您可以清除允许删除的终态活动记录，或使用设置中提供的保留期控制。传输临时文件和恢复检查点按照相应恢复及清理生命周期处理。

卸载会按 Android 规则删除应用私有数据。导出到系统文档位置、其他应用或另一台设备中的副本，需要在相应位置另行删除。

账号与 Google Play 购买

应用没有开发者账号或云同步。0.1.1 及以后版本通过 Google Play Billing 提供可选的终身解锁。Google 处理 Play 账号、结账与付款信息。应用读取商品和购买状态，并确认已完成的购买，不会收到您的银行卡信息。本地权益标记不参与 Android 备份。恢复购买需使用原购买所用的 Google Play 账号；待处理订单不会授予权益。

广告与隐私选择

内部测试版本 0.1.2 接入 Google Mobile Ads 和用户消息平台（UMP），使用 Google 演示广告位。较早的 0.1.0 不含在线内购或广告。测试广告仍会连接 Google 服务。Google SDK 可能处理 IP 地址、广告交互、诊断信息以及 App Set ID 等设备标识；IP 地址可能反映大致位置。应用请求非个性化广告，移除了 Android 广告 ID 权限，并关闭发布商第一方标识。这些措施不代表 SDK 完全不处理数据。

应用在请求广告前检查适用的隐私选择，并在 UMP 要求时提供隐私选项。应用清单、所选文件名称、路径、安装包内容和传输详情不会提供给广告 SDK。激励广告为可选项，只有 SDK 报告奖励完成后才对当前批次生效。本地奖励记录保存不含文件信息的批次标识、数量与消耗状态。Google 服务的数据处理见 Google 隐私政策。

应用没有由开发者运营的远程使用分析或崩溃上报后台。本地统计与 Google 广告衡量相互独立。卸载按 Android 规则删除本地数据，但不会删除 Google 的购买或服务记录；请通过 Google 账号与隐私控制管理这些记录。

支持邮件与本网站

您主动发送支持邮件时，我们会收到您提供的邮箱、正文及附件，用于回应和调查问题。邮件服务会处理这些信息以投递和保存邮件。请勿发送密码、付款凭据、完整应用清单或连接二维码。您可向同一邮箱请求删除支持往来；信息仅在处理请求、解决相关问题或履行适用法律要求所必需的期间保留。

本网站没有广告、分析脚本或文件上传表单。GitHub Pages 及其基础设施提供方为交付和保护网站会处理 IP 地址、请求时间、浏览器信息等技术请求数据。这与应用在设备本地处理数据的方式不同。外部链接和您的邮件应用适用各自服务的规则。

儿童

ApkShare Pro 是通用安装包管理工具，并非专门面向儿童的服务。我们不会通过支持渠道主动索取儿童个人信息。家长或监护人可联系我们，请求删除儿童曾发送的信息。

变更与联系

数据处理方式发生变化时，我们会更新本页，并在需要时提供应用内说明或征求同意。如需提出隐私问题、访问或删除请求，请联系 pjuneye@gmail.com。
