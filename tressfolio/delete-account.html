Delete your Tressfolio account and data

Updated November 21, 2026

NewPower Technology offers account deletion for Tressfolio on Android (studio.tressfolio.hairstyle).

Delete in the app

  1. Open Tressfolio using the account you want to delete.
  2. Open Mine, enter the settings or profile menu, and choose Delete Account.
  3. Review the effects and confirm when ready. This permanently removes account access, profile, credits, and creations. Saved files on your own device can be removed from its gallery.

Request deletion without the app

When our support mailbox becomes available, email the contact below with the subject “Tressfolio account deletion” and the email address or account ID associated with Tressfolio. Do not send a password or a selfie. We may ask for information to confirm ownership before acting.

What happens to the data

Uploaded images and generated results expire within 24 hours of task completion. Records required for legal or accounting purposes may be retained for the applicable statutory period.

Contact

For help today, open Mine and select Feedback. Our designated support email is support@tresssfolio.ai. Email delivery is being set up. Until it is available, use in-app Feedback for support and the in-app deletion option for account removal.
