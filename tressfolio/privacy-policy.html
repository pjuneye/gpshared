Privacy Policy for Tressfolio

Updated November 21, 2026

NewPower Technology operates the Tressfolio Android app (studio.tressfolio.hairstyle). This policy describes its handling of account data, photos, and app activity.

Your photos and previews

When you submit a photo for a template, the app sends the selected image and template parameters to our servers over HTTPS. Photos can include faces. We process the request on our own GPU infrastructure or send it to RunPod, Inc. in the United States for inference. We use the photo to return the requested visual result. We delete the uploaded image and generated result within 24 hours after task completion. We do not use them to train AI models. Copies you choose to save to your device stay under your control.

Accounts, purchases and support

Guest sessions use a device identifier. Email sign-in uses an email address and password, and we maintain an account identifier, profile, activity history and credit balance. If you make a Google Play purchase, Google handles the payment and we use the purchase record to deliver and reconcile credits. Feedback may contain the text and attachments you choose to provide.

Device data and measurement

The app and its services may process device and OS information, app interactions, technical diagnostics, install source and advertising identifiers where available. We use this information for service operation, reliability, fraud prevention and install attribution. Our systems may share relevant identifiers and install or app events with measurement partners, including Meta. Depending on the campaign configuration, these events may include a hashed email address or account identifier, an IP address used to infer country, a device advertising identifier and purchase value. Photos are provided to our inference provider for the requested generation. Google sign-in and AppsFlyer measurement are disabled in the first internal Android release.

Retention and choices

We keep account data while the account is active, support records while needed to resolve requests, and transaction records as required for accounting and legal obligations. You can request access or correction through Feedback, and delete your account in the app. See account and data deletion. The service is for people aged 13 and older. If a child under 13 has submitted information, contact us so we can investigate and remove it.

Contact

For help today, open Mine and select Feedback. Our designated support email is support@tressfolio.ai. Email delivery is being set up. Until it is available, use in-app Feedback for support and the in-app deletion option for account removal.
