Agefolio account and data requests

NewPower Technology operates Agefolio.

Request account or data deletion by email

Email support@agefolio.ai with the subject “Agefolio account deletion”. Say whether you want your account and associated data deleted or want to remove a specific portrait or task. Include the email address linked to your account, or your Agefolio account or task ID if available. Please do not send a password, photo, or payment details.

You can use this email after uninstalling Agefolio. If you cannot identify a guest account, describe your request and we will let you know what information is needed to verify it.

Request deletion in the app

If you have a registered account and still have Agefolio installed, sign in and select Delete Account in your account settings. Guest users can also request deletion through Feedback from their guest session.

Photos and device copies

We delete submitted photos and generated portraits within 24 hours after task completion. Remove any copies saved on your device from its gallery.
