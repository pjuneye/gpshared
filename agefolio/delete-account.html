Agefolio account and data requests

NewPower Technology operates the Agefolio Android internal test. For help, open Feedback in the app.

Guest testers

Open Feedback from the app while using the guest session associated with your request. Say whether you want a particular task removed or the guest account and its associated data deleted. Include the task or account identifier if available. You do not need to attach a photo or password.

Registered accounts

If you registered an account, sign into it and use the Delete Account control in the account settings. You may also request help through Feedback.

Photos and device copies

We delete submitted photos and generated portraits within 24 hours after task completion. Remove any copies saved on your device from its gallery.
