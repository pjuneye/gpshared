Agefolio Privacy Policy

NewPower Technology operates the Agefolio Android app. For help, open Feedback in the app. Effective November 25, 2026.

Photos and age portraits

When you submit a photo, Agefolio sends the image and the chosen age stage to its servers using HTTPS. The image may contain a face. Our servers and the computing providers that run generation process the photo to produce your requested portrait. We delete the uploaded image and generated result within 24 hours after the task completes. We do not use either image to train models. Files you save on your device stay there until you remove them.

Guest sessions and support

A guest session uses identifiers to associate your device with a session, generation requests and works. If you use email account features, the service also processes the address and login information you enter. Feedback includes the message and any attachments you submit.

Device and service activity

The app may send device and operating system information, app version, network address, install source, available advertising identifiers, app events and error information. These support session management, service operation, troubleshooting and install measurement. Depending on the configuration, identifiers and install events may be shared with measurement providers. Agefolio is free to download; optional credit packs can be purchased in the app.

Your choices

Choose which photo to submit. Only upload photos you are entitled to use. You can request access, correction or deletion through Feedback in Agefolio. Include the relevant task or account identifier if available.
