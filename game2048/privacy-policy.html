Game 2048 Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 22, 2026 · Provider: NewPower Studios

This policy explains how the Game 2048 Android app handles information. The app can be played without creating an account and does not request contacts, location, camera, microphone, phone, or storage permissions.

Information handled by the app

Game progress, preferences, scores, Undo credits, and Clear 2 + 4 credits are stored locally on your device. Clear 2 + 4 removes every tile valued 2 or 4; it does not suggest a move. You can delete this local data by clearing the app’s storage or uninstalling it.

If you choose to sign in to Google Play Games, Google processes your player identity, achievements, and leaderboard scores. The app uses that information only to provide those optional features.

Advertising, consent, and diagnostics

The app uses Google AdMob to show native and rewarded ads. Depending on your region and choices, Google and its advertising partners may process device identifiers (including the Android advertising ID), approximate location derived from network information, ad interactions, diagnostics, and fraud-prevention signals. Google’s User Messaging Platform is used to request and remember consent where required. You can reopen available advertising choices from Settings → Privacy choices.

Firebase Remote Config is used only to retrieve an advertising on/off configuration. It may process app-instance and technical request information needed to provide that service. The app does not include Firebase Analytics.

Sharing and retention

NewPower Studios does not sell personal information. Information is shared only with the service providers named above to operate ads, consent, remote configuration, and optional Play Games features. Their retention and deletion practices are governed by their own policies and your account or consent controls. Local game data remains until you clear it or uninstall the app.

Children

The app is a general-audience puzzle game and is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through a third-party service, contact us so we can help route the request.

Your choices

You may play without Play Games sign-in, manage advertising choices when the option is available, reset or limit the advertising ID in Android settings, clear local app data, or uninstall the app. Privacy and deletion questions can be sent to pjuneye@gmail.com.

Security and changes

We use reasonable safeguards and HTTPS for the online copy of this policy, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure. We may update this policy when the app or legal requirements change. The effective date above identifies the latest version.
