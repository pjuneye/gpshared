Privacy Policy for Neon AI

Last updated: April 6, 2026

NewPower Technology ("we", "us", or "our") operates the Neon AI mobile application (the "App"). This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal information when you use our App.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide

  • Account Information: Email address, nickname, and profile picture when you register or sign in via Google or Facebook.
  • Photos and Images: Photos you upload to use our AI-powered image processing features (e.g., face swap, style transfer).
  • Payment Information: Purchase records for credit packages processed through Google Play Billing. We do not store your credit card or payment method details.
  • Feedback: Messages and information you submit through our feedback form.

1.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Device Information: Device model, operating system version, and unique device identifier for guest account management.
  • Usage Data: Features used, templates selected, and task completion status.
  • Log Data: App crash reports and performance analytics to improve our service.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for:

  • Providing and maintaining our AI image processing services
  • Processing transactions and managing your credit balance
  • Creating and managing your account
  • Sending notifications about your tasks and account status
  • Improving our App and developing new features
  • Responding to your feedback and support requests

3. Permissions We Request

  • Camera: To take photos for AI image processing features.
  • Photo Library / Storage: To access and save photos on your device.
  • Internet Access: To connect to our servers for AI processing and data synchronization.

4. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored on secure servers and transmitted using encryption (SSL/TLS). We implement appropriate security measures to protect your personal information. However, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure.

5. Third-Party Services

Our App integrates with the following third-party services:

  • Google Play Billing: For in-app purchases. Subject to Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Sign-In: For authentication. Subject to Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Facebook Login: For authentication. Subject to Facebook's Privacy Policy.

6. Data Retention

We retain your personal data only for as long as necessary to provide our services. Uploaded images are processed and may be stored for a limited period to deliver results. You may request deletion of your data at any time.

7. Children's Privacy

Our App is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have collected such information, please contact us immediately.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction or deletion of your data
  • Withdraw consent for data processing
  • Export your data in a portable format

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.
