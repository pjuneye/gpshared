Delete your Drapose account

NewPower Technology operates Drapose (Android package studio.drapose.lookbook).

Request deletion by email

Email support@drapose.ai with the subject “Drapose account deletion”. Send the request from the address linked to your account if possible. If you use a guest account, include your Drapose user ID if available. Support may ask you to verify ownership. Never send your password.

Delete your account in the app

Open your profile, choose Delete Account and confirm. You can also remove individual generated results from Creations.

What happens to your data

Account information is deleted within 30 days of account deletion. Photos, results and associated metadata are removed on account deletion. Outside account deletion, uploaded photos and results expire within 24 hours of task completion. Purchase records are retained as required for tax and accounting purposes. Feedback is retained while needed to address the request. Crash and analytics logs are kept for up to 90 days and then deleted or aggregated.
