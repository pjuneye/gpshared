Privacy Policy

Last updated: December 14, 2025

1. Introduction

Welcome to Path Board. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

2. Data Collection

We do not collect any personal identifiable information (PII) on our own servers. However, the app uses third-party services that may collect information used to identify you:

  • Google Play Services: For achievements, leaderboards, and cloud save.
  • AdMob: To display advertisements.
  • RevenueCat: To process in-app purchases.

3. Local Data

Game progress, scores, and settings are stored locally on your device. You can clear this data at any time by clearing the app's storage or using the "Delete Data" option in the app settings.

4. Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13.
